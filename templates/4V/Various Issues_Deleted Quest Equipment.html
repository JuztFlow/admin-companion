You can actually recover your deleted +19 quest equipment yourself through the Character Management section on the 4Vision website. First, please visit the Account Management page at https://vision4s.com/manage and make sure you are logged into the correct account.

Then, open the "Character Management" section:


You can also access it directly here: https://vision4s.com/manage/characters

There, you will find a list of all your current and deleted characters. Simply locate the character the +19 quest equipment is intended for and click the "Quest Equip" button next to it (the equipment will automatically be for the class of the character you select):


When doing that, a confirmation dialog will open that informs you that you can only receive the +19 quest equipment once every 24 hours per character:


After you confirm that, the equipment will be delivered to your character in-game within approximately 1 to 2 minutes.
